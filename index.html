About Me

I received my PhD from the Department of Intelligence and Information at Seoul National University in September 2025, advised by Professor Jung Ho Ahn. I am currently a Software Engineer at CryptoLab Inc., where I lead GPU code optimization for homomorphic encryption libraries.

My research interests lie in GPU acceleration and the design of application-specific accelerators informed by profiling and performance analysis of conventional computing platforms. I primarily focus on accelerating cryptographic primitives, including homomorphic encryption, private information retrieval, and zero-knowledge proofs.

I have authored numerous publications in top-tier computer architecture venues (ISCA, HPCA, MICRO), with a cumulative citation count exceeding 1,000.

Research Interests

Computer Architecture

Designing application-specific accelerators through systematic profiling and performance analysis of conventional computing platforms. Focus on memory-centric optimization and efficient hardware utilization.

Homomorphic Encryption

Accelerating fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) operations on GPUs and custom ASICs. Proposed BTS, the first bootstrappable FHE accelerator (ISCA 2022, 246 citations).

Privacy-Preserving Computing

Enabling practical privacy-preserving machine learning and private information retrieval through HW/SW co-design. Bridging the gap between theoretical security and real-world deployment.

GPU Acceleration

Hardware-aware optimization of cryptographic workloads on modern GPU microarchitectures. Achieved over 100x speedup for FHE bootstrapping through memory-centric GPU optimization.
